# Assumptions

The brief leaves a lot open on purpose. These are the calls I made, each with a one-line reason. DESIGN.md and SPEC.md build on them.

History: first pass written by me. A devil's advocate review then found 11 open questions and 17 edge cases. Items marked (DA) were added or changed because of it. A23 and the change to A12 came from the design review.

## The domain

| #   | Question                                                           | My call                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | Why                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| --- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| A1  | How long does a service take?                                      | Fixed per service type, stored on `service_types.duration_minutes` (must be > 0). The appointment stores its own `starts_at` and `ends_at` at booking time. (DA)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | Dealerships quote fixed labour times per job. Storing the end on the appointment means a later change to a duration never silently moves or overlaps existing bookings.                                                                          |
| A2  | What makes a technician "qualified"?                               | A technician has a set of skills. A service type requires exactly one skill. Many to many through `technician_skills`.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | Smallest model that still makes "qualified" a real check. Moving to a set of required skills later means a join table, a migration and a "has all of" query. Not free, but contained in one query. (DA)                                          |
| A3  | Which bays can a service use?                                      | Any bay at the dealership. No bay types (lift, alignment rig, wash) for now.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | Keeps the first version honest and small. Bay capabilities would mirror the skill model and are on the next-steps list.                                                                                                                          |
| A4  | Where do bays and technicians work?                                | Each belongs to exactly one dealership. No sharing across sites. The database enforces that the bay and technician on an appointment belong to the appointment's dealership (composite foreign keys).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      | Matches how most service departments staff, and a cross-site assignment is a bug the schema can refuse for free.                                                                                                                                 |
| A5  | Who picks the technician?                                          | The system assigns a free qualified technician. The request may name a `preferredTechnicianId`. Unknown id: 404. Not qualified or works at another dealership: 422. Busy: 409. No silent fallback to someone else.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | A customer who asks for a named person should not quietly get somebody else.                                                                                                                                                                     |
| A6  | When can work happen?                                              | Opening hours per dealership, one window per weekday, in local time. A booking must start and end inside that window on the same local day. Ending exactly at closing time is fine. No lunch breaks and no windows that run past midnight. Closed days have no window. (DA)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                | The minimum real constraint. One window per day covers how service departments open. Split windows and overnight shifts are rare for service work.                                                                                               |
| A7  | Do technicians have their own shifts?                              | No. A technician is available whenever the dealership is open. Shifts, holidays, sick days and one-off dealership closures are out of scope. (DA)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Would need a roster and a calendar of closures. First on the next-steps list.                                                                                                                                                                    |
| A8  | Time zones                                                         | Each dealership has an IANA time zone. The API takes ISO 8601 times with an explicit offset (`Z` or `+02:00`). A time without an offset gets 400. Everything is stored as UTC `timestamptz`. The end is the start instant plus the duration (real elapsed minutes, not wall clock). Weekday, opening hours and slot alignment are checked in the dealership's zone.                                                                                                                                                                                        | An offset makes every request an exact instant, so there is no guessing. The server's own time zone is never used. Doing the weekday lookup in UTC would check Monday morning in Sydney against Sunday.                                          |
| A9  | Start time granularity                                             | Start times must sit on a 15 minute boundary in the dealership's local time, with zero seconds and milliseconds (configurable step). (DA)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | Matches how service desks book. Rejects 09:07 and 09:00:00.001, which would otherwise end a millisecond late and block the next slot.                                                                                                            |
| A10 | Booking window                                                     | The start must be later than now and no more than 90 days (90 × 24 hours) ahead. Both checked against an injected clock.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | Stops bookings in the past and keeps the search space bounded. The injected clock keeps tests stable.                                                                                                                                            |
| A11 | Gaps between jobs                                                  | None. Ranges are half open, `[start, end)`, so a job ending at 10:00 does not block one starting at 10:00.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 | A buffer can be added to the duration later without changing the model.                                                                                                                                                                          |
| A12 | Customers and vehicles                                             | They already exist (seeded). The API takes their ids. The vehicle must belong to the customer at booking time, otherwise 422. That is checked by the service, not by a foreign key, because cars get sold and an old appointment must not block the change of owner. Creating customers and vehicles is out of scope (they would come from an upstream system).                                                                                                                                                                                            | The brief is about booking, not customer management.                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| A13 | Can one vehicle have two overlapping bookings?                     | No, at any dealership. The database refuses it, like it does for bays and technicians. (DA)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                | A car can't be in two places at once, so this check is not limited to one dealership.                                                                                                                                                            |
| A14 | Appointment status                                                 | `confirmed` or `cancelled` today. Every appointment that isn't cancelled holds its bay, technician and vehicle, so a status added later (say `in_progress`) holds them by default. There is no cancel endpoint yet, but the constraints already ignore cancelled rows.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | Cancel and reschedule are next steps, and adding them should not need a migration.                                                                                                                                                               |
| A15 | Retrying the same request                                          | An optional `Idempotency-Key` header, scoped to the customer. It is saved on the appointment in the same transaction as the booking, so two in-flight requests with the same key can't both book. Same key and same request returns the original appointment. Same key with a different request gets 422. "Same request" compares the parsed fields, with the start time as a UTC instant, so `09:00+01:00` and `08:00Z` match. Only successful bookings are stored, so a retry after a 409 is a fresh attempt. Keys live as long as the appointment. (DA) | Mobile clients retry on flaky networks, often while the first request is still running.                                                                                                                                                          |
| A16 | Which free bay and technician get picked?                          | Bays: lowest id. Technicians: the free qualified one with the fewest skills, then lowest id. (DA)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Deterministic and easy to test. Picking the least flexible technician first keeps generalists free for jobs only they can do. It is a heuristic, not an optimum. Bookings arrive one at a time, so no rule can know what the next request needs. |
| A17 | Who is calling?                                                    | No auth in this version. The `customerId` in the body is trusted. In production it would come from the caller's token.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | Auth is real work that the brief doesn't ask for. Noted in the security section.                                                                                                                                                                 |
| A18 | Does the vehicle change who is qualified or how long it takes?     | No, not in this version. Qualification and duration depend on the service type only. (DA)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | Said on purpose so nobody reads it as an oversight. An EV needing a high-voltage certified technician is the obvious next rule, and it fits the skill model.                                                                                     |
| A19 | Two requests race for the same bay or technician                   | The database decides who wins. The loser looks again and takes the next free pair. A 409 is returned only when no free pair is left, never because of a lost race. With a preferred technician, a lost race on that technician is a 409. (DA)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | A 409 must mean "nothing is free", or the customer gets refused while staff stand idle.                                                                                                                                                          |
| A20 | Temporary versus permanent refusals                                | 409 means "not at this time, try another". 422 means "this request can never work as asked": the dealership has no bay, or no technician with the skill, the vehicle is not the customer's, the time is outside opening hours or off the 15 minute grid. (DA)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | Clients retry 409s. A permanent problem dressed up as a temporary one wastes everybody's time.                                                                                                                                                   |
| A21 | Several services in one visit                                      | One service type per appointment. An oil change and a tyre rotation are two appointments. (DA)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             | Combined visits need a planner that sequences jobs. Out of scope, and noted as a question for the business.                                                                                                                                      |
| A22 | Removing a bay or technician                                       | Not supported. Foreign keys are `ON DELETE RESTRICT`, so a resource with appointments can't be deleted by accident. An "active" flag is a next step. (DA)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | Prevents a delete from quietly removing confirmed appointments.                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| A23 | How big does it get? (my planning estimate, not a measured number) | One deployment serves a network of up to about 1,000 dealerships, each with 5 to 20 bays. A busy dealership books 100 to 150 jobs a day. Peak is around 10 bookings per second across the network, with about 20 availability reads per booking (people browse before they book), so about 200 reads per second. Targets: `POST /appointments` p95 under 300 ms, `GET /availability` p95 under 200 ms.                                                                                                                                                     | Without a number the scale story can't be checked. These are deliberately generous for a dealer network, and still small for one Postgres primary. The load test measures against them.                                                          |

## Out of scope

Cancel and reschedule endpoints, technician shifts and absences, dealership closure days, bay types, combined visits, multi-day jobs, buffers between jobs, payments, notifications, customer and vehicle management, auth, and a frontend. Each is listed as a next step in DESIGN.md where it matters.
