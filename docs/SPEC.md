# Spec: Unified Service Scheduler (backend)

## Goal

A REST API that books a service appointment for a customer's vehicle at a dealership, at the time they ask for. Before confirming, it makes sure a service bay and a qualified technician are both free for the whole service duration, and it never double books a bay, a technician or a vehicle, even when two people book at the same moment. A confirmed booking is stored as an `Appointment` that links the customer, vehicle, technician and bay.

## In scope

- `POST /appointments`, `GET /appointments/:id`, `GET /availability`
- Opening hours per dealership in its own time zone, 15 minute start grid, 90 day booking window
- Automatic assignment of a free bay and a free qualified technician, with an optional preferred technician
- An optional `Idempotency-Key` header
- Structured logs, Prometheus metrics, a health check, an OpenAPI spec
- Seed data for a few dealerships, and a one-command run with Docker Compose

Idempotency, `GET /availability` and the operations endpoints sit below the cut line in [PLAN.md](PLAN.md). Their criteria are under "After the cut line" at the end, so it's clear what the core submission is.

## Out of scope

Cancel and reschedule, technician shifts and absences, closure days, bay types, combined visits, payments, notifications, customer and vehicle management, auth, and a frontend.

## The assumptions that shape the tests most

The full list (23 of them) is in [ASSUMPTIONS.md](ASSUMPTIONS.md). These five change what the tests look like:

1. A service type has a fixed duration and needs one skill. Any bay at the dealership works.
2. Times come in as ISO 8601 with an offset. Opening hours, weekday and grid are checked in the dealership's IANA zone.
3. Ranges are half open, so a job ending at 10:00 doesn't block one starting at 10:00.
4. 409 means "not at this time", 422 means "can never work as asked", 503 means "the server couldn't decide in time, try again" (see "Timeouts and 503" below).
5. The free technician with the fewest skills is picked first, then the lowest id. Bays go by lowest id.

## Order of checks

When a request breaks more than one rule, the first one in this list decides the answer. I fixed the order so two reasonable implementations can't give different codes for the same request.

1. 400 `VALIDATION_FAILED` (the body and headers).
2. The idempotency lookup, if there's a key: replay the original, or 422 `IDEMPOTENCY_KEY_REUSED`. This runs before everything below on purpose, so a replay still works after its start time has passed (ADR 001).
3. 404s, in this order: `DEALERSHIP_NOT_FOUND`, `SERVICE_TYPE_NOT_FOUND`, `CUSTOMER_NOT_FOUND`, `VEHICLE_NOT_FOUND`, `TECHNICIAN_NOT_FOUND` (the preferred technician).
4. 422s, in this order: `VEHICLE_NOT_OWNED_BY_CUSTOMER`, `TECHNICIAN_NOT_ELIGIBLE`, `SERVICE_NOT_OFFERED`, then the time rules `START_NOT_ON_GRID`, `START_IN_PAST`, `BEYOND_BOOKING_HORIZON`, `OUTSIDE_OPENING_HOURS` (grid, past, horizon, opening hours is the order the scheduling module checks them).
5. 409s, in the ADR 001 order: `VEHICLE_ALREADY_BOOKED`, `TECHNICIAN_UNAVAILABLE`, `SLOT_UNAVAILABLE`.

AC-37 tests this order.

## Timeouts and 503

Every booking attempt is its own short transaction at READ COMMITTED. The service sets the level itself on every transaction (`BEGIN ISOLATION LEVEL READ COMMITTED`), so a database whose default is something else doesn't change how booking behaves (AC-38). The transaction also sets `lock_timeout` and `statement_timeout` with `SET LOCAL`, so the settings end with the transaction and it still works behind PgBouncer in transaction mode. The values come from config:

- `lock_timeout` 2 s by default
- `statement_timeout` 5 s by default
- the time budget for the booking loop 5 s by default. It's checked before each attempt: no new attempt starts once it's gone. It is not a hard ceiling on one attempt.
- getting a connection from the pool 2 s by default (`DB_CONNECT_TIMEOUT_MS`)

503 `BOOKING_CONTENDED` with `Retry-After` comes back when the loop hits its attempt cap or its time budget, when a deadlock (40P01), serialization failure (40001) or lock timeout (55P03) keeps happening after 3 retries (4 tries in all), or straight away on a statement timeout (57014). None of these is ever a 409, because they say nothing about free slots (AC-25, AC-41).

Outside the booking loop (the lookups before it, `GET /availability`, `GET /appointments/{id}`), a database that isn't there right now gives 503 `SERVICE_UNAVAILABLE` with `Retry-After`, not a 500: a refused or dropped connection (also one lost in the middle of a request), no free pool connection within the connect timeout, a statement timeout, or a lock timeout, deadlock or serialization failure. A 500 is only for real bugs.

## Acceptance criteria

Every criterion has at least one test, and the test name starts with its id (for example `AC-03 ...`), so `grep -r "AC-03" test/` finds it. Ids are stable. New criteria were added at the end of the numbering, so a few sit out of order in the tables.

### Shared setup

Unless a case says otherwise:

- Dealership **North** in `Europe/London`, open Monday to Friday 08:00 to 17:30 and Saturday 09:00 to 13:00, closed Sunday.
- Dealership **South** in `Europe/London`, same hours as North, with one bay and one OIL_SERVICE technician.
- Dealership **Harbour** in `Australia/Sydney`, open Monday to Saturday 08:00 to 17:00, closed Sunday, with one bay and one OIL_SERVICE technician.
- Dealership **Rhine** in `Europe/Berlin`, open Monday to Friday 09:00 to 17:00, closed at weekends, with one bay and one OIL_SERVICE technician.
- Service types: **OIL** takes 60 minutes and needs skill `OIL_SERVICE`. **BRAKES** takes 90 minutes and needs `BRAKES`. **DIAG** takes 180 minutes and needs `DIAG`.
- "Now" is fixed by the test clock at 2027-03-01 09:00 UTC (a Monday).
- Booking cases (AC-01 to AC-11, AC-18 to AC-20, AC-37) are on **Tue 9 March 2027**. Other dates below are in March 2027 unless they say otherwise. Times are London local unless they carry an offset.
- Fixtures use fixed UUIDs that sort in name order: bay 1 < bay 2, T1 < T2, TA < TB. For example bay 1 is `00000000-0000-4000-8000-000000000001` and bay 2 is `00000000-0000-4000-8000-000000000002`. "Lowest id" in a case means the one that sorts first.
- Existing bookings in a "Given" are inserted as raw rows. They use a vehicle that isn't the requesting vehicle, and they hold only the bay and technician the case names. When a case needs a bay or technician for the existing row that it doesn't name, it says which.

